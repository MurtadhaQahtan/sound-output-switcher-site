Privacy Policy – Sound Output Switcher

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Sound Output Switcher is developed by Murtadha Qahtan (Microsoft Store publisher “rul”).

Sound Output Switcher does not collect, record, store, transmit or share any personal data or audio.

  • Offline only. The app never connects to the internet for its features. It has no analytics, telemetry, advertising, crash reporting, accounts or third-party SDKs. (Microsoft Store handles purchase; the app itself does not phone home.)
  • No data sent. Nothing is collected or sent anywhere. There is no telemetry.
  • Audio devices. The app lists your playback devices and can change which one Windows uses as the default output. It does not record, play back, or send any sound.
  • Settings (shortcut, which devices to cycle, start-with-Windows) are stored in a local file on your PC only and are removed when you uninstall.
  • Children. The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone.
  • Payments are handled entirely by Microsoft Store; the developer receives no payment details.

Política de privacidad (resumen)

Sound Output Switcher no recopila, almacena, transmite ni comparte datos personales ni audio. Funciona sin conexión (salvo la compra de Microsoft Store), sin telemetría ni anuncios. La configuración se guarda solo en tu PC.

Datenschutz (Kurzfassung)

Sound Output Switcher erfasst, speichert, überträgt oder teilt keine personenbezogenen Daten und kein Audio. Die App arbeitet offline (außer dem Microsoft-Store-Kauf), ohne Telemetrie und Werbung. Einstellungen werden nur lokal auf Ihrem PC gespeichert.

Sound Output Switcher は個人データや音声を収集・保存・送信・共有しません。オフラインで動作し（Microsoft Store での購入を除く）、テレメトリや広告はありません。設定はお使いの PC 上にのみ保存されます。
